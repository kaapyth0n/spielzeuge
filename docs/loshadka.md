# Лошадка

Лошадка (Pferdchen, Pony) is Matryona’s third game. She was six when she drew it and told her dad how it works: you make a pony, and a finished pony gives you three stars. Open `/loshadka/` or choose it in the catalog.

## From the interview and the drawing to the game

Her whole explanation: «Здесь надо делать лошадку. Вот с этой стороны слева — гривы разные, сверху заколки, справа хвосты, а снизу цвет лошадки можно поменять. Вот сделаешь лошадку — получаешь 3 звёздочки.»

| Matryona said / drew | In the game |
| --- | --- |

| “Слева — гривы разные”: four manes (yellow-orange, green, blue, pink with flowers) | The left column holds the manes, starting with her four, plus black, white curly, purple braids and chestnut. The pony faces left, like hers, so the mane column is on the side of its head. |
| “Сверху заколки”: fruit clips with leafy tops (a strawberry, an orange, a blue berry, a dark one with a fluffy green top) | The top row holds the clips: strawberry, orange, blueberry and — the fluffy green one — a broccoli clip, then a bow, a daisy, a heart, a star and a butterfly. |
| “Справа хвосты”: black, red, green, blue, orange, pink and a striped one | The right column holds the tails in her colours and styles: flowing, curly, a fluffy pompom, a pink braid with a bow, the striped tail, and a white one. |

Dad asked for a nicer look than a copy of the crayon drawing, so the structure is hers and the drawing style is a soft, polished picture-book style.

## Playing

- **The workshop** is the whole screen. Manes are in the left column, clips in the top row, tails in the right column and the pony's colours in the bottom row. Each row has a small label (Гривы, Заколки, Хвосты, Цвет) that stays at its start while the row scrolls on its own; a soft fade at an end means there is more. On narrow phones the top and bottom labels sit above their rows. All four rows use one tile size, so the frame looks even. The corners hold home (top left), sound and RU/DE/EN (top right), the barn with the star total (bottom left) and the big golden **Готово** star (bottom right).
- **Rows show what matters:** when the workshop opens, each row scrolls to its newest gift (with «Новое!»), otherwise to what the pony wears. A glowing row makes its tiles hop one after another.
- **A new pony is a sketch:** white, outlined, with dashed ghost outlines and little arrows showing where the mane, the tail and the clip go. The dotted outline of the body points down to the colours. When the pony is drawn small (phones), the ghost lines are bolder and the arrows bigger. Tapping a ghost names the missing part and makes its row glow.
- **Picking** from a row fills that part in with a pop and sparkles, the narrator says the item's name and a sound plays in that row's voice (harp for manes, a twinkle for clips, a swish and chime for tails, a paint bloop for colours). The worn item has a golden ring. Tapping the worn item again makes the pony wiggle with joy; nothing is taken off. The horn and the wings switch on and off.
- **Готово** sleeps (softer gold, still pressable) until the pony has a mane, a clip, a tail and a colour. Then it glows and hops, and the narrator says once per pony: «Какая красивая лошадка! Теперь нажми на золотую звезду.» Tapped too early, it boops softly, the first missing row glows and the narrator says where to look (mane, then clip, then tail, then colour).
- **Three stars:** a finished pony earns three stars. They fly from Готово into the three slots in the upper right of the picture, where Matryona drew them, one after another with rising chimes and sparkles; the flight stays inside the picture. The slots sit on a soft dark pill, so empty ones show on light backgrounds too. The pony rears up, hearts fly, a big «Ура!» appears in the empty sky of the picture (above the pony, or beside it on low screens), the narrator says «Ура! Лошадка готова! Вот тебе три звёздочки!», the pony neighs after «Ура!» and the star total on the barn counts up. The next card waits until the line is said (about 4.4 s; 3 s when the sound is off), and a tap in the first 1.5 s only sparkles, so a tap on the rearing pony does not skip the big moment.
- **A name** (new ponies only): a card with the pony's portrait asks «Как зовут лошадку?» and reads the three names out («Облачко, Ириска или Конфетка?»), so a child who cannot read yet can choose. Tapping a name chooses it at once, the narrator says «Привет, …!» and the pony neighs back after the name. The card stays, so every name can be tried; a golden ✓ appears with the first choice and goes on. The dice offers three other names and reads them out the same way. An edited pony keeps its name.
- **The photo:** a polaroid of the pony on its background, taken with a flash and a click, with its name and three stars; the pony introduces itself («И-го-го! Я …!»). Three buttons: «В конюшню» (golden, gently bouncing as the next step), «Новая лошадка», «Ещё наряжать». In portrait they stand in a column.
- **The slots stay golden** while the pony on the stage is the finished one. Change anything and they empty again: pressing Готово then earns three more stars. Only a new pony opens a gift, so switching the horn on and off on one pony cannot empty the gift list. Готово on an unchanged pony says «Эта лошадка уже готова! Поменяй что-нибудь или сделай новую.»
- **A finished pony moves into the stable.** Whenever the workshop opens again (back from the stable, a new visit, a page restored from the back/forward cache), a finished pony that has not changed since is in the stable and the stage holds a new sketch, so making a new pony never quietly changes a stable pony. Only «Ещё наряжать» on the photo and «Наряжать» in the stable keep a finished pony on the stage for editing. A half-made pony, or unsaved changes to a stable pony, stay where they are.
- **The pony is alive:** it breathes, blinks, twitches its ears and swishes its tail. Tap its body: it hops and neighs. Head: «Фыр-фыр!» with an ear wiggle. Hooves: a clip-clop tap dance. Mane, tail or clip: that part shakes and its row glows. Horn: sparkles. Wings: they flap. The apple on the grass: the pony lowers its head and eats it in three bites («Ням-ням!»); a new apple grows after about four seconds. The apple sits right under the lowered mouth; taps on the pony wait while it eats (its head lies on the apple), and a tap on the empty spot while the apple grows back gives a few sparkles.
- **Stage buttons** (upper left of the picture): «?» replays Matryona's explanation while each row glows as it is named; the round picture shows the next background and switches to it (its name is spoken). A freshly opened background comes next, with a «Новое!» badge on the button; after that the backgrounds take turns. «+» starts a new sketch. If the pony on the stage has unsaved changes, the first «+» asks («Эта лошадка ещё не готова. Нажми ещё раз — и начнём новую.») and the button shakes; a second tap within four seconds starts the new pony.
- **Double taps:** every two-tap question («Нажми ещё раз»: «+», «Наряжать», «Отпустить погулять») ignores a confirming tap that comes less than 0.65 s after the question: that is the second half of a double tap, not an answer. A tap that switches screens makes the next 0.4 s of taps count as its second half too, so a double tap on «В мастерскую» never lands on the home link under it. Two quick taps on the background button leave one background, the newest.
- **Keyboard:** every cell and button is focusable with a white ring (berry on cream cards and the sky); a focused cell at a hidden end of a row scrolls the row to it. Enter and Space activate; Enter on the pony is a body tap. While a card is open, the workshop behind it is inert and Tab and Shift+Tab go round the card; sound and language stay usable above it. Escape moves the celebration on (it skips the stars, keeps the offered name, opens the gift, closes the photo) and closes the stable's action bar.

## Gifts

- Every new finished pony opens the next gift, in the order of `GIFTS` in `src/loshadka-items.ts`: unicorn horn, rainbow mane, crown, rainbow tail, the starry night background, wings, the golden pony and so on, 27 gifts in all.
- After the stars (and the name), a wrapped present bounces: «Подарок! Нажми на подарок!». Tapping it lifts the lid, the item rises out of the box with sparkles and the narrator says what it is and where to find it («Рог единорога! Смотри сверху, в самом конце.»).
- The new item joins its row with a pink «Новое!» badge (inside the tile, so a row's edge never cuts it) until it is tapped once. When the photo closes, the row scrolls to the new item, glows, and its tile bounces with sparkles. The horn and the wings sit at the end of the top row; a new background waits on the round background button with its badge.
- Each row ends with up to two wrapped presents for that row's next gifts. The very next gift of all wiggles. Tapping a present says «Это подарок! Сделай лошадку — и он откроется.»

## Stable

- The barn in the lower left corner opens the stable: a sunny meadow with the barn, an apple tree, a pond, flowers and butterflies. Every finished pony lives here, newest in front.
- Ponies wander slowly, graze with their heads down, stand and swish their tails, and sometimes trot. Farther ponies are smaller and behind nearer ones; the chosen pony stands in front of everybody. On phones and in portrait the ponies are drawn bigger, so even far ones are easy to tap. With reduced motion they stand still and only blink.
- Tapping a pony makes it hop, shows its name in a bubble and says «И-го-го! Я …!»; it neighs after its name. An action bar appears under it (above it when there is no room; on narrow phones the buttons show only their icons):
  - **«Наряжать»** brings the pony back to the workshop. Its stars stay in the slots until something changes; Готово then saves the new look and earns three stars. If the workshop holds a half-made pony (or unsaved changes), the first tap asks («В мастерской лошадка ещё не готова. Нажми ещё раз — и будем наряжать эту.»); a second tap within four seconds dresses this pony.
  - **«Отпустить погулять»** asks first («Точно отпустить погулять? Нажми ещё раз.»). A second tap within four seconds and the pony trots off the meadow («Пока-пока, …! Беги гулять!»). For eight seconds a «Вернуть» button calls it back: it turns round (or trots in again from that side) and says «… снова здесь!».
- The top bar has the way back to the workshop, the title (berry on a white glow, readable on the sky), the star total, sound and language; on 320 px phones the title and the star chip shrink so the language button fits. Below it, a small wrapped present says «Следующая лошадка откроет подарок!» (or «Все подарки открыты! Ура!»).
- An empty stable says «Здесь пока никого нет. Сделай первую лошадку!» with a big «Новая лошадка» button, on the grass under the barn (low at the bottom on landscape phones, clear of the gift line).
- At most 30 ponies live in the stable. Готово on a new pony while it is full gives no stars; a card says «Конюшня уже полная! Отпусти одну лошадку погулять — и новая поместится.» with a button to the stable. Editing a stable pony still works.
- After the photo, «В конюшню» opens the stable with the new pony already introducing itself.

## Language, narration and sound

- Russian, German and English share `spielzeuge.lang`; `?lang=ru|de|en` works too. The RU/DE/EN selector sits in the top right corner of the workshop and in the stable's top bar. Changing it speaks the language name, cancels speech in the old language and relabels everything (rows keep their scroll position). Delayed lines (hooray, the name question, the gift) are read in the current language when they play.
- Narration uses `PuppyNarration` with `pronounce` from `src/loshadka-copy.ts` (it respells «Жемчужинка» and «Замок» for Apple's Russian voice). It speaks the welcome (first visit) or «Привет-привет!» (later visits), every item name, ghost and missing hints, allSet, the celebration, names, the gift, background names, pony taps and the stable lines.
- **The welcome tour:** each row glows while the narrator names it. `WELCOME_CUES` in `src/loshadka-copy.ts` holds, per language, when «Слева — гривы», «сверху — заколки», «справа — хвосты» and «а снизу — цвет лошадки» start; they were measured with `say` (Milena, Anna, Daniel) on every prefix of the line and scaled to the narration rate 0.86. Measure again when a welcome line changes. Any tap cuts the welcome short, and its glows stop with it.
- **iOS speaks only after a tap.** If the browser refuses the first visit's welcome (`PuppyNarration.refused`), the game does not count the child as welcomed: the first tap of the sitting tells Matryona's explanation with the glowing rows instead of its own line (the tapped item is still put on).
- Effects are synthesized in `src/loshadka-sfx.ts`, quiet and gentle, one voice per row. The neigh is the one real recording: after the first tap the game loads `/sounds/horse.mp3` (the public-domain neigh Kuckuck uses) once and plays it a little faster (×1.12–1.32, so it sounds like a pony and each pony slightly different), a little quieter, fading out after 1.5 s. Until it has loaded, or if it cannot load, a synthesized whinny stands in.
- Neighs never cover a spoken name: after a name is chosen the pony neighs when «Привет, …!» is said, in the stable after «Я …!», and at the hooray after «Ура!». The next tap cancels a neigh still waiting.
- The sound button mutes speech and effects, stops what is playing and is saved with the game. While muted it wiggles when a tap would have spoken, so a grown-up sees why the pony is quiet. Hiding the page silences everything; leaving it tears down audio, speech, timers and the meadow animation.
- A page restored from the back/forward cache reads the stored game and the language again before it does anything (another Лошадка page may have made ponies meanwhile), then starts a fresh sitting on the workshop. When another tab saves the game, an open page takes that save too.
- Without speech, audio or storage the game still plays. When the device refuses storage, a quiet note says «Лошадки не сохранятся на этом устройстве.»

## How it works

- `src/loshadka-items.ts` — the shared contract: every mane, clip, tail, colour, magic item and background with names in three languages, the gift order and the 28 pony names. Ids never change because they are saved.
- `src/loshadka-state.ts` — pure rules and persistence (`spielzeuge.loshadka.v1`): wear, finish (stars, gift, new or edited pony), names, release, restore from any broken input, `reopenWorkshop` (a finished, unchanged pony moves into the stable) and `nextBackground` (a fresh gift background first).
- `src/loshadka-art.ts` + `src/loshadka-horse.css` — the pony as layered SVG with pivots for every move (hop, prance, neigh, snort, tap dance, munch, walk, trot, graze, part shakes, pop-ins, wing flaps), tap zones (`data-zone`) and ghost outlines; the strip icons.
- `src/loshadka-scenes.ts` + `src/loshadka-scenes.css` — the seven backgrounds, the grassy mound with its apple, the stable meadow, gift boxes, stars and line icons.
- `src/loshadka-copy.ts` — every text in three languages; `src/loshadka-sfx.ts` — effects and the neigh sample.
- `src/loshadka.ts` + `src/loshadka.css` — the screens. Each screen renders its skeleton once; a pick only redraws the stage pony and updates the pressed cells, the slots and Готово, so the rows keep their scroll positions. Rows are rebuilt only for a new gift or a new language, and their scroll is restored. The mound is drawn at 0.82 × the pony's units under its hooves, which puts `MOUND_APPLE` under `HORSE_MUNCH_MOUTH`. Taps on the pony go by `closest('[data-zone]')`; only painted parts of the pony and the apple take taps. The celebration is a small state machine (stars → hooray → name → gift → opened → photo). The stars run by themselves (Escape skips them); the hooray continues by a tap after its first 1.5 s or by itself when its line is said; the name card continues with its ✓; the gift opens by a tap; Escape moves every step on. All its timers die with the screen. While a card is up, the frame behind it is `inert` (except the sound and language corner) and Tab is kept inside the card. The stable is a `requestAnimationFrame` loop of little walkers (walk, trot, graze, stand), depth-sorted and scaled by distance, paused while the page is hidden.
- The layout is one CSS grid over the whole viewport (`100dvh`, safe areas): portrait screens get narrow side columns and tall top and bottom rows, landscape screens the other way round. Tap targets: tiles are 84 px on the iPad and 56–67 px on phones; the stage buttons about 74 px on the iPad and 48 px on phones; home, sound and language at least 44 px (56–76 px on the iPad); pills and the dice 56 px on phones and up to 72 px on the iPad. The stage uses container queries to size the pony, the stage buttons and the star slots.

### Test hook

`window.__loshadka.state()` returns `{ screen, lang, look, missing, complete, done, editing, stars, finished, ponies: [{ id, name, nameIndex, look }], celebration, background, sound, unlocked, nextGift, persistent, selected, neighSample }`. `celebration` is `'stars' | 'hooray' | 'name' | 'gift' | 'opened' | 'card' | 'full' | null`; `done` means the slots are golden (finished, unchanged); `unlocked` lists opened gifts as `kind:id`. `window.__loshadka.save()` returns the stored save. With `?debug=1`, `give(n)` opens the next n gifts and `fill()` dresses the stage pony with the first item of every empty side.

Useful selectors: cells `.lh-cell[data-kind][data-id]` (`aria-pressed`, `.is-new` with `.lh-new`), wrapped gifts `.lh-cell-gift[data-gift="kind:id"]` (`.is-next`), rows `.lh-strip-mane|clip|tail|color` (`.is-glow`), scrollers `[data-scroller="side"]`, the stage pony `.lh-stage .lh-stage-pony` (its svg gets `is-hop`, `is-munch`, …), the apple `.lh-stage .lh-apple` (`.is-eaten`, `.is-growing`), stage buttons `.lh-help`, `.lh-bgbtn`, `.lh-newbtn` (`.is-armed`), slots `.lh-slot` (`.is-filled`), `.lh-done` (`.is-ready` / `.is-asleep`), `.lh-barn` with `.lh-badge-n`, `.lh-sound`, `.lh-lang-select`, the overlay `.lh-layer[data-step]` with `.lh-hooray`, `.lh-name[data-name]`, `.lh-dice`, the name card's ✓ `.lh-card-name .lh-card-next` (hidden until a name is chosen), `.lh-biggift`, `.lh-card-next`, `[data-action="to-stable" | "new-pony" | "keep"]`; in the stable `.lh-pony[data-pony]` (moving: click with `force`), `.lh-bubble`, `.lh-actions [data-action="dress" | "release"]` (`.is-armed`), `.lh-undo` («Вернуть»), `.lh-empty [data-action="first-pony"]`, `[data-action="workshop"]`. Row labels `.lh-strip > .lh-tab` sit outside the scrollers. Mind the double-tap guards when driving the page: wait about 0.45 s after a tap that switched screens and 0.7 s before confirming a two-tap question.

## Checks

- `npm test` covers the modules: item integrity and every rule of the state (restore from garbage, finishing, gifts, names, release, reopening the workshop, the background order), the art (every item and combination renders, ids are prefixed, zones and ghosts are there, bold ghosts), the scenes, the copy (keys, plurals, no typographic quotes, `pronounce`, the names read as a question, the welcome cues) and the effects (every effect schedules valid audio, silence, mute, the neigh sample with its rate, level, fade and fallback). `src/sobachka-narration.test.ts` checks the `refused` signal.
- `npm run test:loshadka` plays the game in Chrome (needs a running server; see the script header for `LOSHADKA_BASE_URL` and `CHROMIUM_PATH`), including double taps on every two-tap question and on screen switches, «Вернуть», the fresh sketch after the stable, the back/forward-cache reload of newer progress and Tab inside the cards.
- The layout was checked with screenshots at 1180×820, 820×1180, 1280×900, 844×390, 390×844 and 320×568 in all three languages: no page scroll, nothing cut off, rows scroll inside themselves, the whole pony stays visible during every move, and every card fits.
