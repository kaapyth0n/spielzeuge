# Тамагочи Связь

Тамагочи Связь (Tamagochi-Funk, Tamagochi Link) is Matryona’s fourth game. She was six when she drew two tamagotchis joined by a dashed line and told her dad how they work. Open `/tamagochi/` or choose it in the catalog.

## From the interview and the drawing to the game

A child does not design a whole game, so her words and her drawing are the starting point and everything else was thought out around them.

Dad asked for a new look, not the style of the earlier toys: the game is drawn in code on one canvas, in a glossy toy style with a thick plum outline. He also asked for it to be fun to play, so the wishes are quick (three wishes take about twenty seconds) and most of the time is spent in the activities.

## Playing

- **Eggs.** A new tamagotchi shows an egg. Three knocks crack it and the pet hatches and says its name.
- **Wishes.** A pet thinks of something in a bubble: food, two poops, the ball or the moon. The first three wishes come in the order Matryona told them (food, poop, ball); later ones are random, never the same twice in a row, and sleep comes at most once between visits. A pink arrow shows the gesture; for the first wishes at once, later after a few seconds. A swipe in the wrong direction makes the pet shake its head and the arrow comes back. A tap on a pet with a wish repeats the wish aloud.
- **Lights.** Each answered wish lights one of the five lights. After hatching and after every visit two lights are on, so three wishes lead to the next visit. Wishes never time out and nothing bad happens to a pet that waits.
- **No wish.** A tap makes the pet do its trick and sing a note in its own voice (the banana spins, the monkey does a somersault); a swipe up makes it hop, a swipe sideways makes it walk.
- **The link.** When a pet has five lights it walks to the right and its antenna glows. With two glowing antennas a ghost line and a finger show what to do. Drag from a glowing antenna (or the shell above the screen) to another tamagotchi: pencil dashes follow the finger, with a rising note for every dash. Letting go on a pet that still has a wish shakes that tamagotchi and names the pet. With three or four tamagotchis there are 2.4 seconds to add more links before the pets set off.
- **The journey.** The pets turn into sparks, run up their antennas, along the dashes and down into the host's screen (the tamagotchi the first line was drawn to), which grows to fill the display.
- **The visit.** The pets stand together in the host's room. Six big buttons start the activities; a star marks the ones never played. Move a pet onto another for a hug. Two activities fit into one visit (two small lights next to the home button count them), then the pets get hungry and go home by themselves. The home button leaves earlier.
- **Friendship hearts.** Every finished activity and the first hug of a visit give one heart. The pill in the top left counts them and shows the way to the next egg: 3 hearts for the mini-tamagotchi, 7 for the bunny, 12 for the little penguin, 18 for the puppy. A tap on the pill says how many are missing.
- **Who lives where.** The small egg button at the bottom of a tamagotchi opens «Кто вылупится?»: every unlocked pet that does not live in another tamagotchi can move in (as a new egg, with two lights). Locked pets are mystery eggs with the number of hearts they need. Each pet keeps its hairdo and accessories when it moves out and back in. With more than two tamagotchis the card can also put the tamagotchi away. The card sits below the corner buttons, so its cross is never under the home button.
- **Things.** Gifts and wire crafts are a shared collection. Wearables (crown, glasses, scarf, bows…) are put on in Причёски; toys and decorations (rug, lamp, picture, garland, clock, plant, ball, teddy…) appear in the tamagotchis' rooms at home.

## The activities

Each activity is a separate file with its own words in three languages, `src/tamagochi-game-<name>.ts`, and works with two, three or four pets.

| Activity | What the child does |
| --- | --- |
| **Танец** | Repeat after your friend: one pet shows a row of moves (swipe up, down, left, right, tap to spin), the child repeats them and the other pets dance. Four rounds, then a free dance where every swipe moves everyone. |
| **Песня** | A well-known tune (public-domain melodies only) is shared between the pets. Notes slide down dashed wires; tap a pet when its note arrives and it sings it in its own voice. A note waits until it is sung. At the end the pets give a concert. |
| **Кольца** | Swipe up to jump, three times each: the mats are trampolines, every jump goes higher, the third is a somersault, and every landing bounces. Rings come down; jump and grab. On the rings: swipe sideways to swing, up to flip, down to stretch, tap to twirl. The ropes are elastic, so the pets bob and wobble, are flung back at the top of a swing, and neighbours that swing into each other bump and fly apart. Each pet has a card of four tricks. They let go with a somersault. |
| **Причёски** | A salon: choose who sits in front of the mirror, then a hairdo (ten), a hair colour (nine, one is a rainbow) and accessories from the collection. The other pet is the hairdresser. |
| **Подарки** | The giver chooses one of three boxes, it flies to the friend along a dashed line, three taps open it. There are 24 gifts; a new one every time until the collection is complete. |
| **Бантики** | Choose a wire colour and a shape (bow and heart at first; star, flower, butterfly, crown and glasses come with practice), trace the dotted line to bend the wire, and choose who wears it. |

## Languages, speech and sound

- Russian, German and English, with the shared `spielzeuge.lang` preference and `?lang=`. The RU/DE/EN button in the top right cycles the language and says its name; the next spoken line is already in the new language.
- Every wish, hint and result is spoken with the device voice and shown as a caption at the bottom. A line is never cut off: the next one waits until the voice has finished (at most two wait; a newer one replaces the second). «Soft» lines (repeated hints) are skipped while another line is being spoken or waiting. The voice is the device's own, set a little higher and livelier than its default.
- All sounds are synthesised: toy bleeps, a voice for each pet that can sing notes (the kitten sings «мяу»), and four quiet looping tunes for the activities. The speaker button mutes speech and sound and remembers the choice (`spielzeuge.tamagochi.sound`).
- Sound starts with the first finished touch: iPad and iPhone allow audio only from the end of a touch, so the frame asks on touch start and on touch end, and asks again after an interruption (a call, the lock screen). The audio session is set to «playback», so the game is heard even with the mute switch on. `/tamagochi/?audio` shows the state of the sound engine in the corner, for checking a device.
- Leaving the page or hiding the tab stops speech and music; coming back through the browser's history starts a fresh sitting from the saved progress.

## Saved progress

`localStorage` key `spielzeuge.tamagochi.v1`: the tamagotchis (pet, hatched, lights), each pet's look, the hearts, the collection, how often each activity was played and how often each hint was shown. Anything broken in storage is repaired or replaced by a fresh game (`parseSave`). Without storage the game still plays and forgets on reload.

## Code

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `src/tamagochi.ts` | The frame: canvas, game loop, fingers (tap, swipe, drag, several at once), language, sound and the corner buttons |
| `src/tamagochi-home.ts` | The tamagotchis: wishes, lights, eggs, the link, the journey, the pet picker |
| `src/tamagochi-meet.ts` | The visit: the room, hugs, the activity buttons, stars and hearts, going home |
| `src/tamagochi-game-*.ts` | The six activities |
| `src/tamagochi-pets.ts` | The eight pets, hairdos, accessories and wire shapes, all drawn from code and posable |
| `src/tamagochi-items.ts` | Food, poop, the ball, gifts and room decorations |
| `src/tamagochi-kit.ts` | Types, palette, drawing helpers, particles |
| `src/tamagochi-sfx.ts` | The synthesiser |
| `src/tamagochi-state.ts` | Saved progress and the rules (wishes, hearts, eggs) |
| `src/tamagochi-copy.ts` | The words of the main game |

Development shortcuts: `/tamagochi/?fresh=1` ignores and does not write saved progress; `?scene=meet` or `?scene=dance|song|rings|hair|gifts|bows` with `&pets=chick,kitten,banana` jumps straight into a visit or an activity.

## Checks

- `npm test` runs `src/tamagochi-state.test.ts`: the fresh game, the heart ladder, the wish rules, repair of stored progress, the words in three languages, gifts and wire shapes.
- `npm run test:tamagochi` plays the game in Chrome against a running server (`TAMAGOCHI_BASE_URL`, default `http://localhost:5173`): hatching, every wish with its gesture, the wrong gesture, the link and a refused link, the journey, the hug, all six activities to their stars, going home, the pet picker with a third tamagotchi, languages, mute, saved progress and five screen sizes. Screenshots go to `tmp/tamagochi/spec/`.

## Not in the game yet

The link joins tamagotchis on one screen, like the two on Matryona’s sheet of paper. Linking two iPads over the network would need a small relay server next to the static site.
