# Living fingerprints / Живые отпечатки

Route: `/prints/`. Original drawn SVG illustrations only: no camera, touch geometry collection, biometrics, uploads, analytics or external art assets.

Choose 1–20 friends (default 8). Each press on the playground stamps a different-coloured, twelve-ridge illustration. The green arrow unlocks after the chosen count. It grows rope-like limbs and eyes, then friends run to eight activities, distributed round-robin: swings, roundabout, slide, climbing wall, cars, monkey bars, sandbox and mini football. The swing uses a shared pendulum transform: the frame stays still while both ropes, the seat and its seated rider move together. Hands hold the ropes and bent legs rest at the seat. One occupant rides; a second stands on the ground and pushes; a third adds a second grounded pusher. Helpers reach for the seat near the bottom of the arc. Further friends wait in separate nearby spots. Roles remain stable until somebody leaves or is dragged; taking the rider off transfers the seat to another friend without duplicate riders. New riders approach the stationary seat before swinging, and an empty swing eases back to rest. Each activity has its own motion; cars have moving wheeled bodies. Mini football has a lined pitch, two netted goals, a rolling ball and alternating kicking poses. The slide uses a continuous 10.4-second circuit: climb the left ladder, cross the platform, follow the curved chute, land, walk around and back along the ground, then return to the ladder. Walking legs articulate with grounded feet. Reassigned friends approach the ladder entrance at bounded speed before starting the circuit; no route-phase teleport. With fewer than eight friends, drag them to explore the other activities. Drag/drop uses SVG coordinate transforms and nearest-equipment targeting, including the empty space around equipment. Pointer cancellation restores the dragged position. A single active pointer prevents competing drags.

Keyboard: focus the playground and use Space/Enter to stamp; activate the green arrow normally; focus and activate a friend, then focus and activate equipment to move that friend. Restart clears the drawing. Native home link returns to the catalog.

Language: visible RU/DE/EN, shared `spielzeuge.lang`, query override. Exact requested Russian prompts appear on-screen and are narrated following a gesture. Replay repeats the current phase prompt. Speech synthesis is device-dependent, with always-visible text fallback; there is no autoplay promise or pre-recorded voice. A persistent sound toggle (`spielzeuge.prints.muted`) controls both narration and quiet synthesized chimes. Language change, hide, restart and exit stop obsolete speech. Exit disposes listeners, ResizeObserver, animation and owned audio; asynchronous audio unlock checks disposal. Restoring a BFCache document starts a new game. Reduced-motion disables decorative character rotation (the seated rider retains essential rotation to stay attached to the ropes) and decorative sun animation; essential activity movement remains.
